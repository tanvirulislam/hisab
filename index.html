What Hisab is

An Android app for tracking everyday income and expenses, budgets, loans and savings. All data stays on the phone. Optionally, you can keep backups in your own Google Drive so the data can be restored on a new or reset phone.

  • No account, no server, no ads, no analytics.
  • Google Drive backup is optional and uses only the drive.file permission: the app can see only the files it creates.
